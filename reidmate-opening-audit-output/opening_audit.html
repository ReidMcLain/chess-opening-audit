Methodology

Games are streamed from the selected files, matched against the supplied aliases after trimming and case-folding, and deduplicated by Chess.com ID/URL when available or by a deterministic player/date/result/main-line-move hash. Unmatched, ambiguous, malformed, and duplicate records do not affect performance statistics.

Opening names come from the pinned CC0 Lichess dataset. The classifier replays legal UCI moves and retains the deepest exact move-prefix or unique normalized four-field-FEN match; literal PGN opening headers are only a fallback. It never derives names from ECO codes or URLs.

Raw score is (wins + 0.5 × draws) / scored games. Rating expectation is 1 / (1 + 10−rating difference / 400). Missing ratings stay in raw W-D-L but not adjusted metrics. Negative actual-minus-expected points mean underperformance.

Smoothed score uses a 8.0-game equivalent prior and the color baseline. If that color has fewer than the prior game count, the overall score is used and recorded as a fallback. Priority is normalized separately for each level and color: 45% non-positive rating underperformance, 35% loss volume, and 20% Bayesian underperformance. Tables never mix White and Black or broad and narrow levels.
